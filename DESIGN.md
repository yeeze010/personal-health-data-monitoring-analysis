# DESIGN.md

## Product Name

个人健康生活网络数据监测与分析系统

## Product Direction

This is a personal health management app, not a hospital command center or generic analytics platform. The primary user is an individual who wants to connect health devices, review personal trends, handle reminders, and selectively authorize family members or advisors.

## Core UX Rules

- Login is required before any profile, health metric, device, family, report, or audit data is rendered.
- Device connection is a first-class workflow: show entries for smart bands, smart watches, blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, Apple Health, Health Connect, and Samsung Health.
- Connection copy should use realistic steps: choose source, authorize scope, sync data, review status.
- Manual records are fallback inputs for data that cannot be synced.
- Use clear button feedback for saving, authorizing, refreshing, completing, and generating reports.
- Avoid enterprise-only wording such as command center, operations cockpit, acceptance platform, or AI analysis.

## Visual Direction

Warm personal health interface with calm surfaces, clear forms, and compact cards. The UI should feel suitable for daily personal use on desktop and mobile, with readable Chinese labels and no decorative AI-style slogans.
